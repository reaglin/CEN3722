CEN3722 — Introduction to Human Computer Interfaces

Module 5

AI-Assisted Design and Development Workflows

This module explores how artificial intelligence tools are reshaping the HCI design and development lifecycle. Topics span AI coding assistants, prompting strategies, rapid prototyping with AI, evaluation methods, and the ethical considerations practitioners must navigate when integrating these tools into professional workflows.
