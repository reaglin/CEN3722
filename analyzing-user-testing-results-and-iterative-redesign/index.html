CEN3722 Introduction to Human Computer Interfaces

Analyzing User Testing Results and Iterative Redesign

This module guides learners through the end-to-end process of making sense of usability test data — moving from raw observations to actionable redesign decisions. Topics span quantitative and qualitative analysis techniques, systematic organization of findings, error classification, and evidence-based prioritization of interface improvements.
