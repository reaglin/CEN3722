Final HCI Evaluation, Reflection, and Lessons Learned

Synthesizes all course concepts by reviewing how analysis, prototyping, and evaluation methods contributed to the quality of each team's final product, with emphasis on connecting HCI theory to real design outcomes. Teams receive structured feedback on design quality, process execution, and collaboration.
