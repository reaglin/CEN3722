CEN3722 — Introduction to Human Computer Interfaces

Final HCI Evaluation, Reflection, and Lessons Learned

This module brings together the full arc of the HCI design process—from theory and analysis through prototyping and evaluation—and guides learners in reflecting on what worked, what didn't, and which lessons can be carried forward to strengthen future interface design practice.

Connecting HCI Theory to Design Outcomes
Topic Detail .html

Explains how HCI theories (such as mental models, affordances, and feedback principles) directly influence concrete design decisions, helping learners connect academic concepts to real interface outcomes.

Review of the Analysis Phase
Topic Detail .html

Reviews the user analysis phase of the HCI design process—covering user research, needs identification, and task analysis—to help learners understand why thorough upfront analysis drives all later design decisions.

Review of the Prototyping Phase
Topic Detail .html

Reviews the prototyping phase of user-centered design, covering types of prototypes, their purpose as testable and disposable representations, and how they enable early assumption-testing and iterative course correction.

Review of the Evaluation Phase
Topic Detail .html

Reviews the evaluation phase of user-centered design, covering usability testing methods, expert review techniques, and how evaluation reveals gaps between designer intent and actual user experience.

Structured Feedback on Design Quality
Topic Detail .html

Covers how to give, receive, and act on structured design feedback from peers, stakeholders, and usability experts, helping learners interpret critique and use it to improve interface quality.

Process Execution and Team Collaboration
Topic Detail .html

Addresses the practical execution of HCI design projects, covering team roles, communication, planning, and documentation practices that determine whether a project succeeds beyond individual technical skill.

Reflection and Lessons Learned
Topic Detail .html

Guides learners through post-project reflection in HCI, explaining how to identify what worked, what failed, and what lessons can be carried forward to improve future design practice.
