CEN3722 – Introduction to Human Computer Interfaces Module 2

CEN3722 — Module 2

Project Scoping and App Platform Selection

This module introduces the foundational practices of defining project scope within an HCI context and evaluating major application platforms—Android, iOS, Windows, and the web—to guide informed design and development decisions. Explore the readings, review the lecture deck, and complete the assignments to build a solid basis for your semester-long group project.
