CEN3722 — Introduction to Human Computer Interfaces

Module 1 — HCI Foundations and Course Project Setup

This module introduces the core principles of Human-Computer Interaction and walks you through the tools and practices that will support your work throughout the course. You will explore foundational HCI theory, establish version-control workflows with GitHub, get started with Figma for collaborative design, survey AI-powered development aids, configure your local environment, and launch your semester team project.
