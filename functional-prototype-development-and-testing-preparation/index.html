CEN3722 — Introduction to Human Computer Interfaces

Functional Prototype Development and Testing Preparation

This module guides learners through the critical transition from low- and mid-fidelity mockups to fully functional prototypes. Topics span design handoff practices, development environment configuration, and the foundational steps required to prepare a prototype for structured usability testing.
