CEN3722 — Introduction to Human Computer Interfaces

Functional Prototype Development and Testing Preparation

This module guides learners through the transition from static design mockups to working, interactive prototypes and prepares them for structured usability testing. Topics span development environment setup, design-to-code handoff strategies, user proficiency surveying, and the creation of rigorous testing protocols — ensuring that prototype builds and test plans are fully aligned before studies begin.

From Mockup to Functional Prototype

This reading explains why static mockups are insufficient for serious design validation and introduces the concept of functional prototypes, covering what makes a prototype 'functional,' the trade-offs involved, and how teams decide when to make the transition from static screens to working, interactive builds.

Transition Strategies from Design to Code

This reading covers strategies for translating polished design mockups into coded prototypes, addressing common pitfalls in the handoff process and providing guidance on how designers and developers can collaborate to maintain consistency between visual intent and functional behavior.

Development Environment Setup and Use

This reading walks through the decisions and steps involved in setting up a development environment for prototype building, including tool selection, collaboration practices, and workflow considerations that teams must address before writing any prototype code.

Designing User Proficiency Surveys

This reading explains how to design user proficiency surveys administered before usability sessions, covering why participant background — such as technology experience, domain familiarity, and prior product exposure — matters and how to craft survey questions that accurately capture it.

Designing Usability Testing Protocols

This reading describes how to design a usability testing protocol, covering the key components of the governing document that structures a user study, why standardization matters, and how to prevent facilitator drift and inconsistency across testing sessions.

This reading addresses the challenge of aligning parallel workstreams — prototype development and test protocol design — so they are fully compatible when testing begins, offering guidance on coordination strategies to prevent mismatches between what the prototype does and what the test expects.
