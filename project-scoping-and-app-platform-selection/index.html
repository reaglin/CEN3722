CEN3722 — Introduction to Human Computer Interfaces

Project Scoping and App Platform Selection

This module introduces the foundational steps of defining a software project's scope and selecting an appropriate target platform. Students will explore platform categories, platform-specific design considerations for Android, iOS, and others, and will begin collaborative group work to produce a formal project scope document.
