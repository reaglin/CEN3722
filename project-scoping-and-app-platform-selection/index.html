CEN3722 — Introduction to Human Computer Interfaces

Project Scoping and App Platform Selection

This module introduces the foundational process of defining a software project's scope and selecting an appropriate target platform. Students will examine platform categories and their design implications — including Android, iOS, and beyond — and apply these concepts through structured group project assignments.
