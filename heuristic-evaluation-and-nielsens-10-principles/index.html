CEN3722 — Introduction to Human Computer Interfaces

Heuristic Evaluation and Nielsen's 10 Principles

This module introduces heuristic evaluation as a practical usability inspection method, examining each of Nielsen's ten usability heuristics in depth and guiding learners through the evaluation process. Use the resources below to read, study, and apply these principles to real-world interface scenarios.
