Heuristic Evaluation and Nielsen's 10 Principles

This module introduces heuristic evaluation as a structured usability inspection method. Learners will explore the definition and origins of heuristic evaluation, examine each of Nielsen's ten usability heuristics in depth, and practise applying the evaluation process to real interface designs.
