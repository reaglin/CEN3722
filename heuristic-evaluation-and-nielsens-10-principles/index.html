CEN3722 — Introduction to Human Computer Interfaces

Heuristic Evaluation and Nielsen's 10 Principles

This module covers the theory and practice of heuristic evaluation as a structured usability inspection technique. You will learn all ten of Nielsen and Molich's usability heuristics, how to apply them to real interface designs, how to rate and document findings, and how to aggregate results across multiple evaluators into a formal report. Use the resources below to read, practice, and reference the module's core content.
