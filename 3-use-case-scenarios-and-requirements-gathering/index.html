CEN3722 — Introduction to Human Computer Interfaces

Module 3 — Use Case Scenarios and Requirements Gathering

This module explores the theory and practice of capturing user needs through use case scenarios and systematic requirements gathering. Learners will study stakeholder identification, elicitation techniques, task analysis, and the craft of writing clear, complete documentation that connects stakeholder intent to what development teams actually build.

Readings & Topic Detail

Topic Detail .html
Introduction to Use Case Scenarios

This reading introduces use case scenarios as a structured narrative technique for capturing user-system interactions, explaining why they exist, what they contain, and how development teams use them to bridge the gap between stakeholder intent and what is actually built.

Topic Detail .html
Identifying Stakeholders and Users

This reading explains how to identify the full range of stakeholders and users connected to a system — including internal roles, external partners, and regulators — and guides learners through the process of mapping who cares about a system before any requirements or use cases are written.

Topic Detail .html
Requirements Gathering Techniques

This reading surveys the major requirements elicitation methods — such as interviews, workshops, observation, and surveys — describing how each technique works, when to apply it, and how analysts use them together to discover and articulate what a system must do and the qualities it must possess.

Topic Detail .html
Task Analysis

This reading covers task analysis as a systematic method for studying how users actually perform their work, including the sequence of steps, decisions, and goals involved, and explains how analysts use this understanding as a foundation for writing accurate use cases and interface designs.

Topic Detail .html
Writing Use Case Scenarios

This reading provides step-by-step guidance on composing well-formed use case scenarios, covering the roles of actors, the structure of main and alternative flows, preconditions and postconditions, and the craft and discipline required to produce clear, complete scenario documentation.

Topic Detail .html
Enumerating Usage Paths and Edge Cases

This reading addresses how to move beyond the 'happy path' by systematically identifying alternative usage paths, edge cases, and boundary conditions, and explains techniques designers and analysts use to enumerate and account for unexpected user behavior and real-world variability.

Topic Detail .html
Documenting and Communicating Requirements

This reading explains how to organize, present, and maintain requirements and use case documents for different audiences — including developers, stakeholders, and testers — covering document structure, version control, communication strategies, and keeping documentation accurate as projects evolve.
