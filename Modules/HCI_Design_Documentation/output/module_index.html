CEN3722 – Introduction to Human Computer Interfaces

HCI Design Documentation and Development Reporting

This module develops the skills required to produce formal, structured documentation across every stage of an HCI design project — from initial requirements gathering through iterative design and evaluation to final implementation reporting. Introduction to HCI Development Reporting
Topic Detail .html

Introduces what an HCI Development Report is, why it differs from informal documentation, and how learners should approach creating a formal structured record of their design project from user needs through to prototype evaluation.

Requirements Documentation
Topic Detail .html

Explains how to capture and document user needs, functional and non-functional requirements, and design constraints, giving learners a guide to building the research and planning foundation that underpins all subsequent design decisions.

Design Process Documentation
Topic Detail .html

Covers how to record design decisions, experiments, and iterative changes throughout a project, helping learners create a transparent, evidence-based account of how and why their design evolved over time.

Evaluation Documentation
Topic Detail .html

Describes how to formally document usability evaluations, including methods used, findings gathered, and how results influenced design revisions, enabling learners to demonstrate that their design choices were grounded in observed user behaviour.

Final Implementation Documentation
Topic Detail .html

Explains how to write the closing section of an HCI project report by documenting what was built, how faithfully it reflects earlier design intentions, and honestly accounting for any gaps between planned and delivered outcomes.

Group Collaboration and Contribution Documentation
Topic Detail .html

Covers how to record team members' individual contributions, collaborative processes, decision-making, and reflections in group projects, helping learners and instructors assess both collective output and each person's role.

Report Structure, Referencing, and Presentation
Topic Detail .html

Guides learners on how to organise a professional HCI report with a coherent structure, apply correct academic referencing, and present the document to communicate a clear, evidence-backed design narrative to evaluators.
