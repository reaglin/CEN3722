CEN3722 — Introduction to Human Computer Interfaces

HCI Design Documentation and Development Reporting

This module introduces learners to the principles and practices of documenting HCI design work and producing clear development reports. Topics span requirements documentation, design-process records, and evaluation reporting, equipping students with the skills to communicate HCI decisions effectively throughout the software development lifecycle.
