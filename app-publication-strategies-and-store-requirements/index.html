CEN3722 — Introduction to Human Computer Interfaces

App Publication Strategies and Store Requirements

This module guides learners through the end-to-end process of publishing applications to major distribution platforms — including Google Play, the Apple App Store, and the Microsoft Store. Topics span developer account configuration, code signing, build preparation, and crafting effective store listings, equipping students with the practical knowledge needed to successfully release software products to end users.
